## My Role

I designed and developed the website rebranding from start to finish as Digizoo’s UI/UX Designer. The project was completed with a tight 2-week turnaround in preparation for inbound leads at the 2026 World Credit Union Conference.

### The Digizoo personal touch

As technology advances, we wanted to show our human side through our website. On the about page, I’ve chosen to use real photos of our team to emphasise that our solutions are bespoke and personalised to each of our clients.

### Time-gated custom hero sections

Hero sections can have start and end times for event-specific campaigns. When the time range finishes, the website automatically redeploys to revert back to the default layout.


<video controls loop muted playsinline width="100%"><source src="/projects/digizoo-website/06.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>


### Performant and component-based for extensibility

The new website is a static website based on the Astro framework. Each page and layout follows the atomic design principle, so components can be reused and extended upon easily.
